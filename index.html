How to read this explorer
  • Inner vs outer belts — inner rings skew hot (Char, Slag, Ingot); outer rings skew cold (Comet, Dewdrop, Soot, Glint, Ember).
  • Site types — Shale, Grove, and Blue Drift mark outer fuel scouting targets in the map data.
  • Trojans — can mix hot and cold rocks even when the map only shows Garden or Annex.
  • Fuel scan — compares Shale vs Grove vs Drift site types; not confirmed in-game odds.
