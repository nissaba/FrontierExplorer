How to read this chapter
  • Inner vs outer belts — the furnace lane favors Char, Slag, and Ingot; the long chill favors Comet, Dewdrop, Soot, Glint, and Ember.
  • Site types — Shale, Grove, and Blue Drift are names the map uses for outer Water Ice scouting; they are not promises.
  • Trojans (hearsay) — inner tags lean hot, outer tags lean cold; temperate hosts sit between the rings, where pilots tell stories about mixed rocks. The Guide repeats them; it does not verify them.
  • Water Ice (nearby list) — labeled Water Ice: No / Low / Medium / High chance from Shale, Grove, and Drift on the map. A scouting hint only, not a promise in your hold.
  • Planets vs sites — planets are on the system map (Barren, Temperate, Ice…). Belts, trojans, L-points, and outer Blue Drift are map sites that may not orbit any listed planet; host refs outside the planet list are anchors only, not “unknown” planets.
